How to read

  • Rows are absorption transitions ordered cool to hot by ionization potential: H I 1216 (Lyα forest sheets & filaments), Mg II 2796, Si II 1260, C II 1335, Si III 1206, C III 977, Si IV 1403, C IV 1548 (metal-enriched filament spines), N V 1239, O VI 1032, Ne VIII 770, and O VII 21.6 Å (Warm-Hot Intergalactic Medium / WHIM shocks and supercluster bridges). Columns compare instrument cases (Layout: Single, Compare R, Compare S/N, Compare both).
  • Flux & velocity: Flux is normalised to the unabsorbed quasar continuum; v is the line-of-sight peculiar velocity relative to the comoving cosmic-web simulation box rest frame (v = 0 km/s).
  • Curves: Stepped line is the degraded spectrum convolved with a Gaussian line-spread function of FWHM = c/R (5 pixels per resolution element) at the chosen S/N per resolution element. Shading shows the noiseless LSF-convolved model; Intrinsic (dashed) shows the native 0.25 km/s optical-depth spectrum before instrumental broadening.
  • Fitted components: In-browser Levenberg–Marquardt Voigt profile fits in ink, with each component drawn in the transition colour and a tick at its velocity centre. A hollow tick marks a component on a parameter bound, cut by the fit time budget, or unconstrained (log N uncertainty above 1 dex). A downward arrow marks a saturated absorber whose log N is a formal lower limit (e.g. “log N > 14.5 sat”).
  • Cosmic-web orientations: Z-axis (box Z) rays probe parallel to the periodic box Z-axis across supercluster and filament planes; XY-plane (filament) rays slice across the primary inter-cluster WHIM filament bridge in the XY-plane; Oblique / 3D rays traverse the 3D cosmic web along reproducible pseudo-random directions across impact parameters b up to 150 Mpc.
  • 3D Cosmic Web: Drag to orbit across 51.7–302.6 Mpc cosmic-web volumes, scroll or pinch to zoom through the 3 nested 256³ levels (L0 full periodic box, L1 Box/4 supercluster nexus, L2 Box/16 WHIM filament bridge down to 12.6–35.9 kpc voxels resolving individual galaxy halos along filaments), and click any sightline to open its 12-ion spectra.

Data and methods

Sightlines through the z = 0 Intergalactic Medium (IGM), Warm-Hot Intergalactic Medium (WHIM), and Cosmic Web across 6 cosmological hydrodynamic simulations (51.7 to 302.6 Mpc periodic boxes).

960 whole-box THOR Voronoi sightlines (160 per simulation box): Exact 3D Voronoi/Delaunay polyhedral cell ray-tracing via official digest-pinned THOR (dataset_type=pointcloud_voronoi, driver_type=raytracer; Byrohl & Nelson 2025) and SBI_CGM FP64 compact Voigt radiative transfer on a 0.5 km/s log-velocity grid resampled to 0.25 km/s native / 1 km/s web grids (TNGS v2 binary format). Custom rays cast in the browser ray-march directly through the nested 3D ion density cubes via the Rust/WebAssembly kernel (fast_viz.wasm).

Ionization: CLOUDY c17.03 (Ferland et al. 2017) photo-+collisional equilibrium under the Faucher-Giguère (2011, FG11) UV+X-ray metagalactic background with Rahmati et al. (2013) H I self-shielding (CLOUDY c17.03 FG11 + Rahmati13 self-shielding (THOR), sfr_temperature = 1000 K), evaluated on each gas cell's density nH, temperature T, and tracked simulation element abundances across all 12 explorer transitions (H I Lyα, Mg II, Si II, C II, Si III, C III, Si IV, C IV, N V, O VI, Ne VIII, O VII).

Atomic data & Voigt fitting: Wavelengths, oscillator strengths f, and damping constants Γ follow Trident 1.4.2 and Morton (2003), with X-ray O VII 21.6019 Å from Verner et al. (1996). In-browser Levenberg–Marquardt Voigt fits optimize (log N, b, v) through the Gaussian line-spread function in a Web Worker, seeded from the intrinsic component catalogue (tngsight/vpfit.py) and pruned by ΔBIC. Saturated components are formal lower limits.

3D 5-level nested Cosmic Web, halo & galaxy starlight cubes: Every gas cell and star particle in each full periodic box is deposited into five nested 256³ cubes: L0 / S0 (±Lbox/2 full periodic box, 51.7–302.6 Mpc, 202–1182 kpc voxels), L1 / S1 (±Lbox/8 = Box/4 supercluster nexus, 12.9–75.7 Mpc, 50.5–295.5 kpc voxels), L2 / S2 (±Lbox/32 = Box/16 WHIM filament bridge, 3.23–18.9 Mpc, 12.6–73.9 kpc voxels), L3 / S3 (±400 kpc central halo / cluster core, 3.125 kpc voxels), and L4 / S4 (±25 kpc central BCG / galaxy disk, 0.195 kpc voxels). Companion 3D galaxy starlight cubes (S0..S4) render SDSS g, r, i Lupton et al. (2004) composites with SKIRT/THEMIS V-band dust optical depth (κV [kpc−1]) encoded in the 4th RGBA channel. 3D velocity cubes store mass-weighted peculiar velocities clipped at the top of the 3D velocity window.
